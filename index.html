About Dr. Keller

A Physician Shaped by
Curiosity & Commitment

Dr. Keller grew up in Connecticut and developed an early passion for science and sport — earning a spot on the Men's Soccer team at New York University while majoring in Biology. After NYU, he continued at the NYU School of Medicine, now ranked among the top medical schools in the country by U.S. News & World Report.

During medical school, Dr. Keller invested significant time in South America conducting medical mission work and research — an experience that deepened his commitment to accessible, patient-centered care. He graduated at the top of his class and was inducted into the Alpha Omega Alpha Honor Society, the highest academic distinction in American medicine.

"From sinus surgery to head and neck oncology, I want every patient to feel genuinely heard — and to leave with a clear path forward."

Dr. Keller completed his residency in Otolaryngology – Head and Neck Surgery at the Medical University of South Carolina, one of the country's most respected ENT training programs. He brought that expertise to Southern Colorado, where he now serves adult and pediatric patients across a wide spectrum of ear, nose, and throat conditions.

Beyond clinical care, Dr. Keller is an active researcher whose work has appeared in peer-reviewed journals including Clinical Otolaryngology, JAMA Facial Plastic Surgery, and Otology & Neurotology. Several of his publications have become widely cited references in the field.

Sinus & Allergy Disorders

Dr. Keller manages sinus disease and allergic conditions across the full spectrum of treatment options—from medical management to advanced surgical techniques. Every patient's plan is personalized to their unique needs and preferences.

Medical Management
Medications & Therapy
Nasal saline rinses, topical and systemic medications, allergy testing, immunotherapy, and medical management of chronic sinusitis. Many patients experience excellent symptom relief with medical therapy alone.
Surgical Options
Minimally Invasive Surgery
Balloon sinuplasty, functional endoscopic sinus surgery (FESS), and endoscopic polypectomy. These advanced techniques provide significant relief for patients who haven't responded to medical therapy.
Your Choice Matters
Dr. Keller discusses all options with you in detail. Together, you'll create a treatment plan that matches your goals and comfort level. You're always in control of your care decisions.

Thyroid & Parathyroid Disorders

Dr. Keller provides comprehensive evaluation and management of thyroid nodules, thyroid cancer, goiter, and parathyroid disorders. Treatment is tailored to your specific diagnosis and personal preferences.

Medical Management
Monitoring & Medication
Thyroid ultrasound, fine-needle aspiration biopsies, TSH monitoring, and medical management with thyroid hormone replacement or other medications. Many thyroid conditions can be effectively managed without surgery.
Surgical Options
Thyroid & Parathyroid Surgery
Partial or total thyroidectomy, parathyroid surgery, and thyroid cancer resection. Dr. Keller performs these procedures with meticulous attention to preservation of vocal cord function and other vital structures.
Your Choice Matters
Whether your thyroid nodule requires surgery or can be safely observed with imaging, Dr. Keller ensures you understand all options and make the decision that's right for you.

Salivary Gland Disease

Dr. Keller specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of salivary gland disorders, including stones, chronic infections, and gland dysfunction. He offers both medical and surgical management tailored to your needs.

Medical Management
Conservative Therapy
Hydration, sialogogues (saliva-promoting agents), warm compresses, and antibiotics for infections. Many salivary gland conditions improve with conservative care and patient education.
Surgical Options
Minimally Invasive Surgery
Salivary endoscopy for stone removal, sialendoscopy to clear duct obstructions, and partial or complete gland resection when necessary. Advanced techniques preserve gland function whenever possible.
Your Choice Matters
Dr. Keller prioritizes preserving salivary gland function. He'll recommend the least invasive approach that addresses your condition and respects your treatment preferences.

Ear & Otologic Disorders

Dr. Keller treats the full spectrum of ear conditions in both adults and children, from hearing loss and Eustachian tube dysfunction to chronic ear disease and dizziness. Care is tailored to your specific diagnosis.

Medical Management
Hearing Aids & Therapy
Comprehensive hearing evaluation, hearing aid fitting and optimization, vestibular therapy for dizziness, and medical management of infections and inflammation.
Surgical Options
Otologic Surgery
Pressure equalization (PE) tube placement, tympanoplasty, ossiculoplasty for conductive hearing loss, cochlear implants, and mastoid surgery. Dr. Keller performs these procedures with excellent functional outcomes.
Your Choice Matters
Whether your hearing loss is best managed with hearing aids, implants, or surgery, Dr. Keller will discuss all options to help you choose the approach that best fits your lifestyle and goals.
🎤

Voice & Airway Disorders

Dr. Keller evaluates and treats vocal cord disorders, hoarseness, swallowing difficulties, and airway problems in patients of all ages. Treatment ranges from voice therapy to advanced surgical intervention.

Medical Management
Voice Therapy & Conservative Care
Referral to speech-language pathology for voice therapy, vocal rest, and behavioral modification. Laryngoscopy to visualize the vocal cords and diagnose underlying conditions.
Surgical Options
Laryngeal Surgery
Vocal cord injection, vocal cord mobilization, laryngeal papilloma removal, and other specialized procedures to restore voice quality and airway function.
Your Choice Matters
Dr. Keller believes voice restoration should be individualized. Together, you'll decide whether voice therapy or surgical intervention is the right path forward for your goals.

Head & Neck Oncology

Dr. Keller has extensive experience diagnosing and surgically treating head and neck cancers, including oral cavity, oropharyngeal, laryngeal, and thyroid cancers. Treatment is coordinated with oncology and radiation specialists.

Diagnosis & Staging
Comprehensive Evaluation
Physical examination, flexible endoscopy, imaging studies (CT, MRI, PET), and tissue biopsy to accurately diagnose and stage cancer. This guides all treatment decisions.
Surgical Management
Surgical Resection & Reconstruction
Precise tumor removal with attention to preserving function and quality of life. Reconstruction with skin grafts or flaps when needed. Coordination with oncology and radiation therapy as indicated.
Your Choice Matters
Cancer treatment is a partnership. Dr. Keller will clearly explain your diagnosis, stage, and all treatment options—surgery, radiation, chemotherapy, or a combination—so you can make informed decisions about your care.
